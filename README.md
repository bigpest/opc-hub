# OPC-Hub: Stories & Tactics for One-Person Companies

OPC-Hub is an open-source knowledge hub for solo founders, indie makers and builders who want to run profitable one-person companies.

The idea started from my own startup journey. I once followed the traditional playbook: write a pitch deck, chase investors, plan to hire a team and build a huge product upfront. Months passed, I burned savings, built features no one wanted, and hit burnout hard. That failure forced me to rethink everything: what if I could build and operate a sustainable business alone?

I began testing the one-person company model. No payroll, no board meetings, no internal politics — just me, users and a tight stack of tools. I learned hard lessons: validate before building, ship the smallest usable version, charge early, use AI to cover gaps in design, marketing and operations, protect my focus, and avoid scaling for scaling’s sake.

This repository collects all those hard-won tactics, case studies, decision frameworks and raw founder stories. Inside you will find idea validation workflows, MVP checklists, pricing templates, solo marketing channels, legal & admin notes, burnout prevention routines, and case studies from other successful OPC builders.

This repo is for you if you want to build a business that serves your life, not the other way around. It is anti-hype, pro-profit, and built for people who prefer execution over fundraising. Contributions are welcome: share your wins, your failed experiments and your own solo founder story.

## What's inside

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [stories/](stories/) | First-hand accounts from solo founders — what they tried, what it cost, what worked |
| [tactics/](tactics/) | Single-operator tactics: validation, pricing, distribution, legal & admin, burnout |

Start with the folder you need. Every content file is plain Markdown and links to related files, so you can read the repo as a set of connected notes rather than a linear book.

## Share your story

Welcome to OPC-Hub! You are warmly invited to share your one-person company stories, practical tactics, hard-won lessons and real business results here. Whether it is a successful case, a painful failure, a useful workflow, a pricing strategy or an operational trick, all authentic sharing is highly valued.

Submit your contribution via a [pull request](CONTRIBUTING.md) or open an [issue](https://github.com/bigpest/opc-hub/issues/new/choose) for discussion. If you prefer to reach me directly, send an email to **guquanyou@yeah.net**.

## Contribute

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first. Short version:

- Share a story or tactic via the issue templates.
- Fix typos and broken links with a direct pull request.
- No ads, affiliate links or product pitches; no personal data about other people.

Pull requests are reviewed in batches, usually once a week.
